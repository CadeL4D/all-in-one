Pocket Ruins

An original village sim. Your villagers live their own lives — you place the buildings, set the job counts, and watch.

Progress saves itself at dawn.

How to play

  1. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom. Your eight villagers are already busy around the camp.
  2. Build a Well for clean water, then a Farm so farmers grow food, and a Sawpit for wood.
  3. Open Jobs to set how many villagers hold each job. They walk, chop, plant, and haul on their own.
  4. Watch the bubbles. A hungry villager eats from storage; a tired one sleeps — if they have a Home.
  5. Wanderers arrive most days. They join if you have supplies and work to give. Housed couples raise children.
  6. The blight comes at night. Corruption spreads in the wilds and its nests spawn raiders. Wall your village and leave ONE gap: raiders take open paths, so the gap funnels them past your Sentry Tower instead of into your walls.
  7. Box the corruption in and Threat rises — the red bar means bigger, meaner nights. Let it sprawl and the pressure fades.
  8. You are the god here. Open God to spend influence — it regrows with your population. Lightning strikes one raider; the Sentry Tower's bolts pierce husks but blots and wraiths shrug them off, so raise a Storm Pylon. Grab lifts anything: fling embers into water, carry wanderers to safety. Meteor devastates — anything in the circle, yours or theirs.
  9. Towers drink bolts. Sawpits fletch them from stored wood — keep both flowing before a long night. Sling Towers instead throw raw stone: crushing damage that cracks bonewalkers, at the price of your wall material.
  10. Refine what you harvest. Sawmills turn wood into boards, Stonecarvers turn stone into blocks, Kitchens turn food into meals — one meal fills a worker whole. Boards and blocks raise the finer buildings.
  11. Raise the camp. Each tier lifts your build limit, storage, range, work speed, and wanderer draw. Tap the camp and raise it — the climb is the game.
  12. Faith is your power. Influence's ceiling is what your people believe you're worth. Shrines and seen miracles raise belief; witnessed deaths and grabbed villagers lower it. A doubting village is a poor god.
  13. Watch the sky. Some nights the moon changes everything: a Full Moon means raiders rise but never march — cull them, because tomorrow's raid doubles. An Eclipse turns midday into a siege. A Blood Moon spawns red bloodlings inside your walls — storm magic answers them. A Meteor Shower rains burning stone on everything, yours included.
  14. Play well, earn Boons. Everything you do feeds god experience; every earned pick buys a permanent boon from the God panel. Boons outlive villages — loss keeps them.
  15. The world is wider than this island. Raise the camp to a Settlement and the Map opens: fund a caravan, send settlers, found a second village in harsher wilds. Migrate folk between your regions — pop 15 or more, healthy adults only.

Everything saves on its own — leave anytime, one day at 2× is a three-minute session.
