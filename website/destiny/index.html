    WELCOME, KEEPER

    A living village.
    A watchful hand.

    01 · Find your clearingChoose a building, tap the map to preview, then Confirm. Drag to move your view; pinch to zoom. Rotate irregular buildings to fit them together.

    02 · Give them a homePlace a Hearthhold, then cottages, a well, and fields. Villagers walk to jobs and carry goods home. Trails speed their journeys.

    03 · Keep the work movingMark trees and stone with the harvest brush. A Beamwright or quarry marks nearby deposits automatically. Change worker priorities in Village.

    04 · Prepare for duskRaids begin on day 3, then every other day. Towers need stone to fire. Walls shape enemy routes. Don’t seal your workers inside.

    05 · Lend a handDeliveries generate influence. Mend damaged buildings, call a Starfall on enemies, or renew timber with Wildseed.

    First chapter: shelter eight villagers, build a field, well, and garden, and reach day 4 with supplies. Keep growing after the milestone.
