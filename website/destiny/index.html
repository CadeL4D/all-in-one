    WELCOME, KEEPER

    A living village.
    A watchful hand.

    01 · Find your clearingChoose a building, drag its preview into place, then Build. Use two fingers to pan or pinch to zoom. Rotate irregular buildings to fit them together.

    02 · Give them a homePlace a Hearthhold, then cottages, a well, and fields. Villagers walk to jobs and carry goods home. Trails speed their journeys.

    03 · Keep the work movingPaint over trees and stone with the harvest brush. Lift to send workers; Undo removes the last stroke. A Beamwright or quarry marks nearby deposits automatically. Change worker priorities in Village.

    04 · Prepare for duskYour difficulty sets the first raid and its pace. The Village panel forecasts the next wave and its stone cost. Towers need stone to fire. Walls shape enemy routes. Don’t seal your workers inside.

    05 · Lend a handDeliveries generate influence. Mend damaged buildings, call a Starfall on enemies, or renew timber with Wildseed.

    First chapter: shelter eight villagers, build a field, well, and garden, and reach day 4 with supplies. Keep growing after the milestone.
