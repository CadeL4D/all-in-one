    A FIELD GUIDE TO SMALL BEGINNINGS

    Build a place worth protecting.

    Place a Hearthhold on clear ground. Six villagers will build it. Add houses, a well, a field, and a timber yard. Citizens choose jobs, walk real paths, and carry their harvest back to the hearth.

    Harvest: click trees or rocks to mark them. Timber yards mark nearby trees automatically. Paths speed walking. Buildings need a reachable edge.

    Build: select a card, rotate with R, and click. Materials are paid up front; workers then construct it. Removing a structure returns half its cost. The hearth cannot be removed.

    Stay ready: from day 3, small raids approach at dusk every other day. Towers spend stone to fire. Walls divert enemies but can trap workers. Food and water are consumed each day; shortage reduces morale and work speed.

    Milestone: shelter eight villagers, build a well, field, and garden, and reach day 4. Continue growing afterward.

    Controls: drag / WASD to pan, wheel or +/− to zoom, R rotates, Space pauses, Escape inspects. Arrow keys move a tile cursor; Enter places or selects. Touch: tap to place, drag to pan. Save locally with the Save button; autosave runs every 20 seconds.
